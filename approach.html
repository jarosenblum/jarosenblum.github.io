Approach

I've designed learning with technology since 1995: first as an instructional designer working with faculty, then as a faculty member teaching instructional systems design, game-based learning, and learning technologies. I hold a Ph.D. in Curriculum and Instruction (Learning Technologies) from UT Austin. Today I coordinate the LEAD Certificate, teach in McCombs' IROM department and in UT's signature course program, and co-edit Artificial Intelligence in Education.

The builds on this site grow out of that work. I use AI tools to make the things I would otherwise only describe, then hold them to the questions I ask as a researcher: who is this for, what evidence supports it, and where does human judgment stay in charge.
