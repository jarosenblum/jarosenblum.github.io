What this portfolio shows

Everything here is something I built, most of it in the past year with AI tools as building partners: a grading assistant that drafts rubric feedback and shows its evidence, an exam pipeline that traces each question back to a lecture, a first-year AI literacy course whose schedule and rubrics come from one course model, a mindfulness app that combines touch, sound, and motion, and a tutorial that teaches researchers responsible AI use.

Each case says what I built, for whom, and how. Every case has screenshots. Where an app is public you can try it, and the grading assistant can be downloaded with a key. The Approach page connects the builds to my research on AI literacy, assessment, game-based learning, and how people adopt new technology.
